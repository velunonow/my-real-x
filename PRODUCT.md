# My Real X — Product

## What it is
A personal, calm collection of the people, posts, videos and articles I chose — and a place to turn them into things I make. It replaces scrolling X's "For You" feed.

## Who it's for
One person (me), on my phone first, laptop second. Not a pro designer. Wants to learn, then create.

## The job
1. **Collect** — save anything worth keeping in one tap: a link, a thought, a person.
2. **Read a little** — a short daily list (5 cards), then stop. Opening a card counts as reading it.
3. **Make something** — every read can become a takeaway, an idea, a draft, a post.
4. **Keep my people few** — new people start on a 14-day trial; keep or drop.

## Principles
- **Finite, never infinite.** Every list ends. Today holds 5 things. No feed, no timers.
- **One box for everything.** Capture never asks for more than one input; details are optional and added later, in place.
- **Making beats reading.** The interface always offers the next thing to make.
- **Mine.** Data is a JSON file in my repo. No accounts, no X API.

## Mode
Operate (a personal tool) with Experience moments: capture and "done for today" are allowed to be expressive; lists stay calm and scannable.

## Not in scope
Feeds, recommendations, notifications, social features, dark mode (light only by choice).
